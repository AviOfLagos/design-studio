# Build in public (optional)

Contents: [The question](#the-question) · [What you produce](#what-you-produce) · [Draft posts](#draft-posts) · [Conversations to join](#conversations-to-join) · [Asking for feedback](#asking-for-feedback) · [Limits](#limits)

## The question

Ask once, in the same message as the mood-board ask (Phase 0), so it costs no extra round trip:

> One more: do you want me to draft a few posts about what you're building, and point you at the
> conversations where people are already talking about this problem? Say no and I'll stick to the
> build.

- **No, or no answer:** skip this file entirely. Don't produce it, don't mention it again.
- **Yes:** produce `docs/SOCIAL.md` from `assets/SOCIAL.md`, after Phase 2 (it reuses the problem
  research) and update it at the end of Phase 7 with what was actually shipped.

## What you produce

`docs/SOCIAL.md`, three sections, short enough to read on a phone:

1. **Draft posts**: 4–6 ready-to-copy posts.
2. **Conversations to join**: 5–10 live threads with links and a suggested reply angle.
3. **Asking for feedback**: 2–3 ways to get people to try it and say what they think.

## Draft posts

The point is to get people talking, not to sell. A post that asks a real question or shows a real
decision beats an announcement.

| Kind | Shape | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **The decision** | what you chose, what you gave up, ask how others did it | "We dropped the signup form. You can use it first and make an account when you save. How did you handle this?" |
| **The problem** | one finding from problem research, in plain words | "Read 60 reviews of budgeting apps. The top complaint isn't features, it's re-entering the same bank login every week." |
| **Before / after** | one screenshot pair, one number | "Same home page. 1,314 words → 292." |
| **Work in progress** | something unfinished, with a specific question | "Two directions for the empty state. Which one tells you what to do?" |
| **Shipped** | what changed for the user, one line, the link | "You can now undo a sent invoice for 30 seconds. [link]" |
| **Thanks / credit** | name the person or thread that changed your mind | "Changed the onboarding after @someone's thread on drop-off. Here's what we did." |

Rules:

- Tweet-length (under 280 characters) or 2–4 short lines. A thread only when there are real images
  to carry it.
- One idea per post. Concrete numbers and screenshots over adjectives.
- No hype words ("revolutionary", "game-changing", "excited to announce"), no hashtag strings, at
  most one emoji.
- Write in the owner's voice: first person, as they'd say it out loud.
- Mark which screenshots from Phase 7 each post should carry.

## Conversations to join

Reuse the Reddit, X and forum links gathered in `problem-research.md`. For each, give:

| Thread | Where | Why it's relevant | Reply angle | Quote or reply |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| link | r/… · X · HN | they're stuck on problem #2 | share how you solved it, ask what they tried | reply |

Prefer threads from the last 30 days with real discussion. A reply should be useful even if the
reader never clicks the link: share the fix or the finding, mention the product only if it actually
answers their question. Never suggest posting the same text in many places.

## Asking for feedback

2–3 concrete suggestions, picked to fit the product. For example:

- A post asking a specific question with two screenshots ("A or B?").
- Offer early access to the people in *Conversations to join* who described the exact problem.
- A pinned "what should we build next?" post linked to the public issue tracker or a feedback form.

## Limits

- **Nothing is posted automatically.** The skill drafts and recommends. It never posts, replies,
  likes, follows or messages anyone, even if a posting tool is connected, unless the owner asks for a
  specific post in their own words at that moment.
- No fabricated metrics, testimonials or user quotes. Every number in a draft comes from the build or
  the research, and every quote is linked.
- Without a browser tool: write the drafts, leave *Conversations to join* as search queries the owner
  can run, and mark it **unverified**. Never invent a thread link.
